# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Internal developers and contributors of the Celestia monorepo. They open the showcase on a simulator, device, or web build while working on `@celestia-project/mobile` — browsing components, verifying props and states render correctly, and catching visual regressions by eye.

## Product Purpose

A workbench for the Celestia Mobile component library: every one of the ~130 primitives, composites, and layouts rendered with realistic sample data, grouped by role, quick to navigate, and honest about each component's states (loading, empty, error, disabled). Success looks like a developer finding any component in a few taps and trusting what they see.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, dense, tool-like. The components carry the color; the chrome stays out of the way. Three words: workbench, precise, unobtrusive.

## Anti-references

Marketing demo apps — no hero banners, no gradient polish, no pitch-deck gloss. Also avoid over-padded galleries that waste a screen on one component and force endless scrolling.

## Design Principles

1. **Components are the content.** Showcase chrome (headers, nav, dividers) is scaffolding, never decoration.
2. **Realistic data everywhere.** Sample content should look like a real product (names, amounts, dates), so components are judged as they'll actually appear.
3. **Show every state.** Loading, empty, error, disabled — a component is only verified when its states are visible.
4. **Findable in seconds.** Predictable grouping, jump-to-component navigation, consistent rhythm between demos.
5. **Practice what the library preaches.** The showcase itself must satisfy the library's own rules: 44pt targets, contrast floors, token-driven theming.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Sensible defaults inherited from the library: WCAG AA contrast, 44pt minimum touch targets, reduced-motion respect. No additional known user needs.
